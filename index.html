Beauty Blueprint Consulting & Systems

Build a business that works without burning yourself out to keep it running.

Running an aesthetics business takes more than being good at what you do. It takes the right mindset, sound business decisions, and systems that work for you.

Our consulting helps solo aestheticians work through the challenges holding them back, improve how they run their business, and put the right systems in place to support sustainable growth.

Section 1

Sometimes, it's not that you're doing too little. You're carrying too much.

You can be fully booked and still feel financially stretched. You can love your work and still dread everything that comes with running the business. And sometimes, the problem isn't just your schedule.

You're struggling to set boundaries.

You accommodate last-minute changes, answer messages after hours, and feel uncomfortable enforcing policies because you don't want to lose clients.

You're working harder without feeling more secure.

You're constantly busy, but you're unsure whether your prices are right, whether your business is profitable, or how to make things more sustainable.

You're the system holding everything together.

Bookings, deposits, client forms, reminders, and follow-ups all depend on you remembering to do them. Taking a break feels like letting something slip.

These challenges aren't solved by working longer hours or downloading another app. They require looking at both how you operate your business and how you operate within it.

Section 2

Work on the business. And the person running it.

Sustainable growth requires more than better tools. It requires the right decisions, boundaries, and business processes to support the way you want to work.

01

Business Mindset & Owner Development

Uncover and dismantle the limiting beliefs, guilt, and fear around money that lead to underpricing, over-accommodating, and working in constant survival mode. Build deep confidence in your decisions, set unshakeable boundaries, and redefine how you see your worth as a business owner.

02

Business Operations & Workflows

Streamline every touchpoint between you and your clients. From non-refundable deposits and client intake to appointment reminders and policy enforcement, we structure how your business operates so it protects your time and income.

03

Pricing & Profitability

Examine your service prices, treatment times, and product costs to eliminate guessing. We align your menu pricing with your true worth and actual profit margins so you feel confident and justified in every dollar you charge.

04

Implementation & System Management

We don't just give you recommendations and leave you to figure it out alone. We put the right operational systems in place and actively manage them for you on an ongoing basis—taking care of the daily administrative work so your business runs smoothly without adding to your workload.
